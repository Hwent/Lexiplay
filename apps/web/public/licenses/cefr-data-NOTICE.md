## C2 selection

Source: Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2 v1.0, created by Octanove Labs, published by Open Language Profiles.

Source CSV: https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj/blob/d4e45b75b38f27b30dfc5c44d8c571aec7e7092f/octanove-vocabulary-profile-c1c2-1.0.csv

Publisher's explicit licence notice: https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj/blob/d4e45b75b38f27b30dfc5c44d8c571aec7e7092f/README.md

Source and adapted C2 selection: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .

LexiPlay modifications: C2 rows selected, spellings trimmed and normalized, slash-separated alternatives expanded, duplicates merged, one explicitly identified `remonstrate:vern` POS typo corrected to verb, existing curated starter retained, and additions restricted to published family matches with current dictionary/POS definitions. Source notes identifying a particular meaning are quarantined pending sense review. The source labels are learning-list evidence, not an official CEFR certificate. No Octanove definitions were imported.

The starter selection consulted ESL Lounge: https://www.esl-lounge.com/student/reference/c2-cefr-vocabulary-word-list.php . Its original explanatory text and entire curated list are not reproduced.

## Family mapping

Source: Paul Nation, BNC/COCA word-family lists v1.0.0; Victoria University of Wellington.

Archive: https://www.wgtn.ac.nz/lals/resources/paul-nations-resources/vocabulary-analysis-programs/range/BNC_COCA_25000.zip

University resource terms: https://www.wgtn.ac.nz/lals/resources/paul-nations-resources . That page offers CC BY-SA 4.0 or GPL v2/3 as appropriate to the resource. The archive has no separate data-specific licence notice. LexiPlay applies CC BY-SA 4.0 to the adapted word-list mapping on that basis; the bundled executable is neither used nor distributed.

Modifications: only explicit headword/member relations in general lists 1–25 are used; source forms are normalized to lower case; only terms represented in LexiPlay's CEFR collections are retained. Missing mappings are left explicit rather than guessed. Frequency bands are not CEFR levels. Each member retains its existing spelling/POS/CEFR evidence and dictionary-entry identity.

Adapted family mapping: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .
